night reading protocol
🌙
Wind-down reading, not cramming. Read 30 min after your last university task ends — give your brain a gear-shift. Avoid reading within 45 min of sleep if it's heavy math (it raises cortisol).
📝
The 3-note rule. After each session write exactly 3 bullet points: one thing you learned, one thing confused you, one question. This forces active recall without journaling guilt.
⚡
Saturday is sacred. Every Saturday, implement something — even tiny. A bouncing ball, a simple AABB check, a pattern in code. This is where the books actually stick.
🎯
Chapter targets, not page targets. Don't count pages — count chapters. Aim for 1–2 chapters per week. Dense chapters may take the full week. That's normal and fine.
🔄
Overlap phases by 2 weeks. Start the next phase's first book before finishing the current phase's last. It eases transitions and keeps momentum from stalling.
📅
Exam buffers built in. Your exam periods (typically Dec and May) should be reading-free. Treat them as automatic pause points — the schedule absorbs up to 3 months of breaks without falling behind significantly.
