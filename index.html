night reading protocol
🌙
Wind-down reading, not cramming. Read 30 min after your last university task ends. Avoid heavy math within 45 min of sleep.
📝
The 3-note rule. After each session write one thing learned, one thing that confused you, one question. Forces recall without journaling guilt.
⚡
Saturday is sacred. Every Saturday, implement something — even tiny. This is where books actually stick.
🎯
Chapter targets, not page targets. Aim for 1–2 chapters per week. Dense chapters may take the full week. That's fine.
🔄
Overlap phases by 2 weeks. Start the next phase's first book before finishing the current phase's last to keep momentum.
📅
Exam buffers built in. Treat Dec and May exam periods as full pauses. The schedule absorbs up to 3 months of breaks.
